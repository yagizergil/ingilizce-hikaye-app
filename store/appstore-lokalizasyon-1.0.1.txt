--- PROMOTIONAL TEXT (170)  -  145/170 ---
New: 63 original stories from A1 to B1, with studio narration. The word being read is highlighted as you listen — beginners can start on day one.

--- WHAT'S NEW (4000)  -  969/4000 ---
This release is entirely about fixing the reading experience.

READER
• The text no longer changes on its own and the page no longer jumps back and forth on the last page of a chapter. The "Next chapter" button is reliably tappable again.
• Lines clipped at the bottom of pages are fixed. Some lines were never shown before moving on; you are no longer skipping any sentences.
• Paragraph spacing now matches the design.
• Changing the typeface, text size or margins keeps you on the sentence you were reading instead of starting over.
• The reading surface no longer blanks out while settings are applied.

LAUNCH AND SETUP
• Fixed getting stuck on the native language step.
• The trial offer that users taking the level test never saw is now shown.
• Numbers are no longer counted as words in the first reading step, so there is always something to tap.
• The app now opens under all conditions.

If you reported a reading bug: thank you, the videos genuinely helped.
